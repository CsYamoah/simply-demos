No app, no deposit page, no phone tag. It's a text thread, and the same thread is where you'll send me reference photos later.

    Tell me what you want done and a couple of days that work. @marigold.studio.frisco on Instagram works too.

    Usually the same day, often within the hour if I'm between clients. Pick one and you're on the book.

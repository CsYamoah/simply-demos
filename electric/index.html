Questions we get on the phone

Anything else, call. Danny or Luis answers, not a menu.

Do you charge to come out?

$79 for a diagnostic visit anywhere in Lubbock. If you go ahead with the repair, the $79 comes off the bill. Quotes for panel upgrades, EV chargers and lighting are free.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Often not. Most 200-amp homes can take a 40 to 60-amp charger circuit. We do a load calculation on site and show you the numbers before recommending anything bigger.

Do you pull permits?

Yes, for every job that needs one, and the permit fee is in your written price. We schedule the City of Lubbock inspection and meet the inspector so you don't have to take off work.

Can I pay over time?

On panel upgrades and generator installs, yes: 12 months same as cash for approved credit, or a longer plan if you need it. Ask when we give you the written price.
